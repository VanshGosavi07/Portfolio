"Brain-Computer Interface in Lie Detector"

ICNGC-2K25 • International Conference on Next Generation Computing

Author: Vansh Gosavi

Published

This research presents an innovative approach to enhance lie detection accuracy by integrating Brain-Computer Interface (BCI) technology. The study focuses on neural signal decoding and analysis to improve the reliability of traditional polygraph systems through advanced signal processing techniques and machine learning algorithms.
